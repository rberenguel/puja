# Audio credits

The generative soundtrack samples and library were copied from
[shotgun-pollock](https://github.com/rberenguel/shotgun-pollock), which in
turn credits:

- Idea for random jazzy music from [Plan8 Music](http://plan8.co) (via their
  Jazzkeys toy). Used previously in Garbuix and Weave.
- Piano samples from [tambien/Piano](https://github.com/tambien/Piano),
  drawing on the excellent Internet Archive piano samples.
- Drum samples from [The Salamander drum kit](https://sfzinstruments.github.io/drums/salamander/)
  by Alexander Holm.
- Audio engine: [Tone.js](https://tonejs.github.io).
